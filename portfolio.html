Beyond writing code

I'm deeply passionate about building and evolving scalable systems. For me that goes far beyond code: it's understanding the architecture, the business model, how users actually interact with the product, choosing the right technologies — and anticipating what breaks when traffic grows.

I like designing independent, loosely-coupled components that are easy to maintain and scale with demand. And I love sharing what I learn: presenting both the problems I've solved and the ones still open, so colleagues with different expertise can challenge my thinking.
